How to read a formation

A formation is shorthand for how eleven outfield players occupy the pitch, written back to front: defenders–midfielders–forwards. The goalkeeper is assumed and never counted. 4-3-3 means four defenders, three midfielders, three forwards — the same eleven bodies, arranged for a different fight.
